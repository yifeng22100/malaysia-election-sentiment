12–14 Aug 2026
GE16 positioning

UMNO-PAS cooperation floated in Pahang, then BN reaffirms unity government with PH

Over three days: Wan Rosdy, as Pahang UMNO chief, said UMNO was open to cooperating with PAS in the state for GE16 "if it benefits the people," though no formal talks had begun; Pahang PAS commissioner Rosli Jabar responded that PAS and UMNO were "almost equal strength" in the state and that the Menteri Besar's post itself could be up for discussion, adding PAS was willing to set aside its goal of leading Pahang outright. Wan Rosdy then said, as Pahang BN chairman, that BN wants its unity government with PH to continue through GE16 — read as BN testing both a PAS option and its existing PH partnership simultaneously.

8–17 Jan 2026
Coalition friction

Wan Rosdy's "DAP easier to work with than MCA" remark sparks BN friction

Wan Rosdy said he found it easier to work with DAP's exco members than with MCA, citing DAP's support for state initiatives, while stressing political differences shouldn't divide Malays and Muslims. MCA secretary-general Chong Sin Woon publicly criticised the remark as unfair and sending the "wrong signal." Days later Wan Rosdy separately accused PN of "perception politics" ahead of GE16.

14 Feb 2024
Exco reshuffle

Pelangai by-election winner sworn into exco; three portfolios restructured

Following Amizar Abu Adam's swearing-in as executive councillor, Wan Rosdy restructured three exco portfolios: Amizar took over Felda Affairs, Cooperatives and Entrepreneurship, while another assemblyman moved to Local Government, Housing, Environment and Green Technology — a minor reshuffle preserving the 9 BN–2 PH exco balance.

The Star · New Straits Times

17 Aug – 7 Oct 2023
By-election

Exco member dies in plane crash; BN survives a defection scare to retain the seat

Exco member Johari Harun (Pelangai) died along with seven others in a light-aircraft crash near Shah Alam on 17 August 2023, triggering a by-election. In the run-up, PN chairman Muhyiddin Yassin said a Pahang government change was possible if BN reps defected after a PN win, while DPM Ahmad Zahid Hamidi countered with a claim that PN assemblymen would defect the other way; Wan Rosdy denied any BN defections were coming and floated reviving the state's anti-hopping bill. Analysts dismissed both claims as by-election posturing. BN's Amizar Abu Adam won on 7 October with a 2,949-vote majority, keeping the seat BN has held since 1986.

Malay Mail · Sinar Daily · The Star

12 Apr – 19 Oct 2023
Legislation

Pahang's anti-party-hopping bill stalls, never tabled

After the state speaker said an anti-hopping bill was expected in May 2023, Wan Rosdy postponed tabling at the Opposition's request, since the government lacked the two-thirds majority needed alone. By 19 October — even after the Pelangai by-election defused the immediate defection scare — tabling remained "under discussion." No further report of the bill being tabled, debated or enacted was found through 2026; it appears to have stalled indefinitely.

Malay Mail · The Star

2 Dec 2022
Government formed

BN-PH unity government forms; exco sworn in 8 BN, 2 PH

Following the hung 2022 Pahang state election (BN 17, PN 17, PH 8), BN and PH agreed to govern together and Wan Rosdy Wan Ismail (UMNO) was reappointed Menteri Besar. Ten assemblymen — eight BN, two PH — were sworn into the exco days later, cementing the coalition's power-sharing structure for the term.

Context

Pahang is one of BN's most reliable strongholds, governed continuously by UMNO-led administrations for decades with only brief interruptions. Wan Rosdy has led the state since May 2018, and the state's political trajectory has largely tracked BN's broader national fortunes rather than generating its own distinct storylines.

No election has been called and none is expected imminently — this state's term does not require a dissolution until 29 Dec 2027 at the earliest edge of its constitutional window.
