Context

Pahang is one of BN's most reliable strongholds, governed continuously by UMNO-led administrations for decades with only brief interruptions. Wan Rosdy has led the state since May 2018, and the state's political trajectory has largely tracked BN's broader national fortunes rather than generating its own distinct storylines.

No election has been called and none is expected imminently — this state's term does not require a dissolution until 29 Dec 2027 at the earliest edge of its constitutional window. See the Overview page for how this fits into the wider 2025–2028 state election cycle, and the Parties page for background on BN-UMNO.
