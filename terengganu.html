The current assembly

Terengganu has not voted in this cycle. Its assembly still sits as the 2023 Terengganu state election left it.

PN won every one of the 32 seats — a clean sweep, and the only single-coalition assembly in the country.

16 Jul – 9 Aug 2026
Coalition realignment

Terengganu Bersatu leadership implodes after the national PAS-Bersatu split

Terengganu Bersatu chief Razali Idris (exco member, Kijal) resigned as state Bersatu chief on 16 July, followed by two more resignations — Bersatu's state information chief and state treasurer — all saying they remained party members but were stepping down from leadership posts and retaining their assembly and exco seats. Razali denied rumours he was defecting to Muhyiddin rival party Pejuang; analysts suggested he was positioning himself as "PAS-friendly." Bersatu's Terengganu No. 2 also quit days later.

Sentiment

Syariah court canes two couples; rights group raises due-process concerns

The Terengganu Syariah Court sentenced two couples to caning — one pair for repeat khalwat, the other for "preparation for illicit sex" — after both were unable to pay fines and had no legal representation; the sentences were carried out at Marang Prison on 7 June. Rights group SIS Forum Malaysia publicly raised proportionality and due-process concerns.

MACC charges exco member with abuse of power over sons' hiring

Federal relations

Samsuri tables Budget 2026, calls unpaid petroleum royalty a "black episode"

Samsuri tabled a RM1.998 billion-revenue state budget and disclosed that RM1.32 billion in petroleum royalties for 2024–2025 had not been directly channelled to the state account — saying that since a 2022 change to the distribution method, Terengganu had only been allowed to manage roughly 50% of royalties owed for 2023–2025, a cumulative shortfall he called "an episode that scars the rights of Terengganu's people."

Legislation

Takzir enactment tightened: missed Friday prayers now risk two years' jail

Previously, punishment under the Shariah Criminal Offences (Takzir) Enactment applied only after three consecutive missed Friday prayers. The amended law applies after a single missed prayer, with penalties up to two years' jail or an RM3,000 fine, enforced via mosque monitoring and community reporting — drawing international coverage.

First public caning under Malaysian syariah law carried out at a Kuala Terengganu mosque

A man convicted of repeat khalwat was publicly caned at Al-Muktafi Billah Shah Mosque, witnessed by around 70 people — the first time a Malaysian state carried out caning openly rather than within prison walls. SUHAKAM condemned it as a human-rights violation; a PAS spiritual leader subsequently urged other PN-run states to adopt the practice too.

Federal relations

Terengganu rejects the Territorial Sea Act amid an oil-royalty dispute with Putrajaya

After Samsuri raised unpaid oil-royalty arrears in Parliament and PM Anwar defended the federal government's fulfilment of state allocations, the state assembly unanimously passed a motion rejecting the Territorial Sea Act 2012, arguing it improperly restricts Terengganu's maritime resource rights under the Federal Constitution. The PMO and Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil called the rejection "cynical" and said the Act remained valid nationwide regardless.

Context

Terengganu flipped from BN to PAS at GE14 in 2018 and has remained under PAS-led PN governance since. Petroleum royalty payments from the federal government ("wang ehsan") are a recurring point of friction between the state and Putrajaya, a dynamic that echoes Sabah and Sarawak's resource-revenue disputes on a smaller scale.

No election has been called and none is expected imminently — this state's term does not require a dissolution until 24 Sep 2028 at the earliest edge of its constitutional window.
