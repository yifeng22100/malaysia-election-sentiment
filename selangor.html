Selangor

Not part of the active 2025–2028 election cycle yet — Selangor's assembly term runs into 2027–2028. A signal log below tracks political developments in the state as they happen, even this far from an election.

Selangor has not voted in this cycle. Its assembly still sits as the 2023 Selangor state election left it.

PH held the state but PN made substantial gains, taking 22 seats in Malaysia’s wealthiest and most populous state.

31 Jul–13 Aug 2026
Governance

Bankruptcy claim against an ADUN triggers a seat-status review — no breach found

Unconfirmed reports that a Selangor assemblyman had been declared bankrupt prompted the state government to review whether the seat's status was affected. Menteri Besar Amirudin Shari said a decision was expected within the week as the state legal adviser prepared a note on the matter, and the Assembly Speaker was asked to clarify the assemblyman's status. On 13 August, Speaker Lau Weng San confirmed the review had concluded: no assemblyman had breached the conditions required to retain their seat, so no by-election is needed.

GE16 positioning

Amirudin warns BN against being "swallowed" by PN ahead of GE16

Speaking as Selangor MB and PH election director in the wake of BN-PN's strong Negeri Sembilan showing, Amirudin Shari urged BN not to let itself be absorbed into a PN-led "ummah unity" push — a reference to Hamzah Zainudin's "Perikatan Barisan Nasional" branding proposal — and said PH remained open to cooperation with any party ahead of GE16. An analyst characterised the offer as a tactical move rather than a genuine realignment signal.

Free Malaysia Today · The Star · The Sun · Bernama

Coalition friction

Selangor MIC chairman says Amirudin has held only "limited dialogue" with BN

Selangor MIC chairman Shanker Raj Ayengar publicly claimed Menteri Besar Amirudin Shari had engaged BN state leaders only sparingly since an initial meeting. Amirudin's aide disputed this, saying he regularly engages BN's state chair Megat Zulkarnain Omardin and BN's exco representative Rizam Ismail — a rare, on-record airing of PH-BN coordination friction inside the state government.

Tengku Zafrul denies by-election or MB ambitions in Selangor

Amid continued speculation that a Selangor by-election could be engineered to install him as Menteri Besar, Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz explicitly denied this was ever a factor in his party switch from UMNO to PKR, saying no such discussions had taken place.

GE16 positioning

Tengku Zafrul joins PKR; Selangor PH rallies publicly behind Amirudin

Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz formally resigned from UMNO on 30 May 2025 to join PKR, reviving speculation about a Selangor MB change. In direct response, Selangor PH leaders issued a public show of unity the next day, affirming Amirudin Shari would remain Menteri Besar for the full term — a rare, explicit internal-coalition reassurance move.

Oct 2024 – Jan 2026
Corruption probe

MBI sand-mining corruption scandal draws a sustained MACC investigation

Starting mid-October 2024, the MACC raided premises tied to a sand-mining concession awarded under Menteri Besar Selangor Incorporated (MBI, the state's investment arm), arresting a former MBI COO and a businessman and seizing or freezing roughly RM6 million in cash and assets. MACC said it was probing 20 MBI-linked development projects; it stated the case did not implicate the sitting MB. The investigation continued through 2025, and by January 2026 The Star reported 13 MBI-linked companies had completed anti-corruption compliance plans as a remedial response — no senior politician had been publicly charged as of that report.

Governance

Exco reshuffle speculation surfaces and is downplayed

Reports suggested Amirudin Shari was planning to swap out two state exco members ahead of an August–September 2024 deadline. Amirudin downplayed the speculation, saying change was "always possible" depending on circumstances, but no reshuffle was confirmed at the time.

11 May 2024
By-election

PH holds Kuala Kubu Baharu in the state's first by-election of the term

Following the death of incumbent Lee Kee Hiong, PH's Pang Sock Tao won the Kuala Kubu Baharu state by-election with a 3,869-vote majority over PN's Khairul Azhari Saut, on a comparatively low 61.5% turnout. It was read as a confidence marker for the unity government the year after the 2023 general election.

Malaysiakini · The Vibes · Media Selangor

Mar–Jul 2024
Defection / anti-hopping test

Selat Klang seat saga becomes a first real test of the anti-hopping law

Selat Klang assemblyman Abdul Rashid Asari, elected on a Bersatu/PN ticket, declared support for MB Amirudin Shari's PH government in March 2024, prompting Bersatu to expel him. Selangor Speaker Lau Weng San ruled on 11–12 July that this amounted to a "constructive expulsion" but that there was insufficient evidence to declare the seat vacant under the state's anti-hopping enactment, letting Rashid keep his seat. PN's Azmin Ali called the ruling partisan and threatened legal action.

The Star · The Malaysian Insight · Scoop · Bernama

5 Feb – 6 Mar 2024
Legislation

Klang elevated to Royal City status; assembly passes enactment bill

Sultan Sharafuddin Idris Shah proclaimed Klang Municipal Council the "Royal Klang City Council" on 5 February 2024, and the state assembly passed the Klang Royal City Council Enactment Bill 2024 on 6 March to give the elevation legal effect — a rare state-level constitutional/administrative act tied directly to the Sultan's institutional role.

Context

Malaysia's most populous and wealthiest state, and a PH stronghold since it first fell to the opposition in 2008. Amirudin Shari has led the state since 2018, and Selangor's scale and economic weight make it one of the most closely watched states in the country whenever it eventually goes to the polls.

No election has been called and none is expected imminently — this state's term does not require a dissolution until 19 Sep 2028 at the earliest edge of its constitutional window.
