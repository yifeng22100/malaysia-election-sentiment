The current assembly

Perlis has not voted in this cycle. Its assembly still sits as the 2022 Perlis state election left it.

PN took 14 of 15 seats in Malaysia’s smallest assembly. The Menteri Besar post has since changed hands within PN — from PAS to Bersatu in December 2025.

9–11 Jul 2026
Coalition stability

MB and an exco member quit Bersatu's Supreme Leadership Council; defection rumours denied

Menteri Besar Abu Bakar Hamzah and exco member Marzita Mansor resigned from Bersatu's Supreme Leadership Council (MPT) — not from the party itself — saying the workload of running Perlis left no bandwidth for MPT duties. The resignation fuelled rumours Abu Bakar was preparing to defect, which he firmly denied, stressing he remains a loyal Bersatu member and the party's Perlis chairman; Muhyiddin Yassin downplayed the move, saying Bersatu remained strong and Abu Bakar remains its Perlis chief.

9–10 Jun 2026
Coalition stability

Perlis government affirms it will stay intact despite the national PAS-Bersatu split

Despite PAS severing political ties with Bersatu nationally, Perlis PAS — holding 6 of 15 assembly seats to Bersatu's 5 — said it would keep backing MB Abu Bakar Hamzah's government, and Abu Bakar said Perlis would remain under Perikatan Nasional until the term ends. Analysts framed it as PAS prioritising state governing stability over the optics of the national rupture.

8 Jun 2026
Coalition realignment

PAS ends cooperation with Bersatu nationally, citing the Perlis "coup" as a key trigger

PAS president Abdul Hadi Awang announced PAS was ending formal political cooperation with Bersatu — the most serious rupture in Perikatan Nasional's history — explicitly naming what PAS called a "political coup" in Perlis (the December 2025 MB switch) as a central grievance, alongside disputes in Negeri Sembilan and Kedah.

27 Jan – 3 Feb 2026
Party discipline

PAS reinstates the three sacked Perlis assemblymen

PAS's Ulama/Syura Council approved appeals from the three assemblymen expelled in December to have their party membership restored, framing the reversal in terms of forgiveness. Secretary-general Takiyuddin Hassan said the three would need to formally reapply as new members; the reinstatement did not restore them to the exco, which remained Bersatu-only.

1–8 Jan 2026
Government formed

New exco sworn in with Bersatu members only, as PAS boycotts in solidarity with the ousted MB

PAS stayed out of the new state exco in solidarity with ousted MB Shukri Ramli, forcing new MB Abu Bakar Hamzah to govern with only four Bersatu assemblymen, each holding four to six portfolios — a direct structural consequence of the December crisis. PAS said the boycott was not meant to challenge the palace; the MB separately drew criticism for suggesting the sacked PAS reps could be considered for exco posts if they regained party membership.

Free Malaysia Today · New Straits Times · Malay Mail

22–28 Dec 2025
Government

Eight MLAs withdraw support; Shukri Ramli resigns, Abu Bakar Hamzah sworn in as MB

Five Bersatu and three PAS assemblymen submitted statutory declarations withdrawing support from then-MB Mohd Shukri Ramli (PAS) to the Raja of Perlis on 22 December, amid a leadership dispute between the two PN component parties. PAS's central working committee expelled the three PAS signatories on 24 December — since Malaysia's anti-hopping law exempts members expelled by their own party, they kept their assembly seats despite losing PAS membership. Shukri resigned on 25 December citing health conditions, and Bersatu's Perlis chief Abu Bakar Hamzah was sworn in as Menteri Besar three days later; Hadi Awang later alleged the crisis was driven by a power struggle over unspecified projects.

Second exco reshuffle drops two Bersatu incumbents

Two sitting Bersatu exco members were dropped from the Perlis exco and replaced by another Bersatu duo. Muhyiddin Yassin defended the move as correcting an earlier oversight in how Bersatu's Perlis appointments were made, saying the change was about party hierarchy rather than performance.

Context

Malaysia's smallest state by population, Perlis has a history of unusually volatile Menteri Besar changes driven by defections and internal coalition disputes. It came under PN control following the 2022–23 political realignment, and most recently saw its Bersatu and PAS components clash directly: predecessor Mohd Shukri Ramli (PAS) stepped down on health grounds on 25 Dec 2025 amid a leadership dispute, and Bersatu's state chief Abu Bakar Hamzah was sworn in as Menteri Besar three days later.

No election has been called and none is expected imminently — this state's term does not require a dissolution until 19 Dec 2027 at the earliest edge of its constitutional window.
