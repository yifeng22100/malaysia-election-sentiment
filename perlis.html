Context

Malaysia's smallest state by population, Perlis has a history of unusually volatile Menteri Besar changes driven by defections and internal coalition disputes. It came under PN control following the 2022–23 political realignment and is currently led by Bersatu's Abu Bakar Hamzah.

No election has been called and none is expected imminently — this state's term does not require a dissolution until 19 Dec 2027 at the earliest edge of its constitutional window. See the Overview page for how this fits into the wider 2025–2028 state election cycle, and the Parties page for background on PN-Bersatu.
