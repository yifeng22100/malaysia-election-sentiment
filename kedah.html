Kedah

Not part of the active 2025–2028 election cycle yet — Kedah's assembly term runs into 2027–2028. A signal log below tracks political developments in the state as they happen, even this far from an election.

The current assembly

Kedah has not voted in this cycle. Its assembly still sits as the 2023 Kedah state election left it.

PN swept 33 of 36 seats, consolidating PAS’s hold on the state under Menteri Besar Muhammad Sanusi Md Nor. Two of those PN assemblymen — Dzowahir Ab Ghani (Suka Menanti) and Abdul Razak Khamis (Sungai Tiang) — were expelled from Bersatu in May 2026 over alleged breaches of the party's code of ethics; MB Sanusi said both remain independent assemblymen backing the state government and keep their existing exco and deputy speaker posts, so the governing majority is unaffected.

24 Jul – 4 Aug 2026
Controversy

Sanusi's race-related campaign remarks trigger a police investigation

Campaigning for PN in Negeri Sembilan, Sanusi told a ceramah crowd that Malays have "no homeland other than Malaysia" while other communities "have India," "have China," and so on. Police confirmed reports lodged against him and said they would summon him under the Penal Code and Communications and Multimedia Act; BN chairman Ahmad Zahid Hamidi warned him against stirring racial sentiment, while Sanusi sent legal notices to PH figures over what he called distorted characterisations of the speech.

Free Malaysia Today · The Star · New Straits Times · South China Morning Post

9 Jul – 10 Aug 2026
Interstate dispute

Kedah-Penang lease-payment dispute escalates again

Building on a 2024 demand to raise the federal honorarium paid to Kedah over historic Penang cession from RM10 million to RM100 million a year, Kedah said it was ready to legally challenge Penang's lease rate; Penang CM Chow Kon Yeow said he would only respond once Kedah filed a formal claim. Sanusi then said Kedah might hold off filing until Perikatan Nasional controls the federal government — explicitly tying the fiscal claim to partisan politics.

Malay Mail

9 Jun 2026
Coalition realignment

PAS ends cooperation with Bersatu nationally; Kedah confirms no reshuffle

After PAS formally ended political cooperation with Bersatu — the biggest fracture inside Perikatan Nasional since its founding — Sanusi said Bersatu exco members could remain in his administration as long as they kept backing the PAS-led state government. No cabinet reshuffle followed.

Malay Mail · The Star · New Straits Times · South China Morning Post

24 Aug 2025
Court ruling

Sanusi wins RM670,000 defamation judgment against the Home Minister

The Alor Setar High Court found Home Minister Saifuddin Nasution liable for defaming Sanusi with 2023 remarks calling him a "thief" over alleged rare-earth-element theft, awarding RM600,000 in damages plus RM70,000 costs and ordering a published apology. Saifuddin's team filed an appeal in September and sought a stay of execution.

Free Malaysia Today · Malay Mail · New Straits Times

10 Dec 2024 – 24 Feb 2025
Court ruling

Sanusi cleared of both sedition charges over 2023 monarchy remarks

Sanusi had been charged in 2023 with sedition over remarks about the Selangor Sultan and the then-Yang di-Pertuan Agong, made during a talk on the Selangor MB appointment and the unity government's formation. The High Court acquitted him of the first charge in December 2024; the Attorney-General's Chambers dropped the second in February 2025, closing out roughly 18 months of legal jeopardy over his most quoted controversial remarks.

Malay Mail · Free Malaysia Today

8 Nov 2024
Federal relations

Sanusi apologises for past harsh remarks against Putrajaya

Sanusi conceded some of his earlier statements against the federal government were harsh and had hurt feelings, expressing hope for forgiveness and noting a "smooth administrative atmosphere" with Putrajaya — a rare conciliatory turn from a MB known for combative federal rhetoric.

Malay Mail

25 Aug – 18 Dec 2024
Legislation

Kedah assembly passes bill placing the Syariah court under the state constitution

The state assembly unanimously passed the Kedah State Constitution (Amendment 2024) Bill, establishing a Syariah Premier Court and elevating the Syariah court's constitutional status, alongside amendments to the Islamic Law Administration and Islamic Family Law enactments. The resulting enactment received royal assent on 18 December 2024.

Sanusi's former political secretary charged with bribery in rare-earth mining case

A former aide to Sanusi was charged with soliciting or accepting bribes linked to a Kedah rare-earth-element mining project — giving the long-running REE controversy a concrete criminal-court dimension beyond the political sparring over it.

Defection

Bersatu expels two PN assemblymen; both stay on as independents backing the government

Bersatu sacked Dzowahir Ab Ghani (Suka Menanti, State Agriculture, Plantation and Transport Committee chairman) and Abdul Razak Khamis (Sungai Tiang, Deputy Speaker) under the party's constitution over alleged breaches of its code of ethics. Menteri Besar Muhammad Sanusi Md Nor said both men remain in their government posts as independent assemblymen who continue to back the state administration, so no reshuffle is needed.

Context

Kedah has been governed by PAS as part of Perikatan Nasional since the 2020 political realignment. Sanusi Md Nor is one of PN's most prominent and outspoken state leaders, frequently generating national headlines for combative remarks well beyond routine state governance.

No election has been called and none is expected imminently — this state's term does not require a dissolution until 25 Sep 2028 at the earliest edge of its constitutional window.
