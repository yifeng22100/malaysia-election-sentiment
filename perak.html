Perak has not voted in this cycle. Its assembly still sits as the 2022 Perak state election left it.

"China flag" controversy at Teluk Intan draws in Perak's opposition leader

Video of a group waving PRC flags during a temple parade at Teluk Intan's Leaning Tower on 24 October 2024 triggered a national furore and 16 police reports. Context

Perak has been governed since December 2020 by Saarani Mohamad, whose administration became a cross-coalition "unity government" spanning BN, PH and GPS-aligned assemblymen after the 2022 state election reaffirmed that arrangement — a structure that mirrors the federal unity government formed the same year, making Perak one of the more direct state-level tests of how durable that arrangement is.

No election has been called and none is expected imminently — this state's term does not require a dissolution until 19 Dec 2027 at the earliest edge of its constitutional window.
