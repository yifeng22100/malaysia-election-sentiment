Context

Penang has been governed by DAP-led administrations continuously since 2008, first under Lim Guan Eng and since 2018 under Chow Kon Yeow. As Malaysia's semiconductor and electronics manufacturing hub, the state's economic profile gives its politics an outsized national profile relative to its size.

No election has been called and none is expected imminently — this state's term does not require a dissolution until 29 Aug 2028 at the earliest edge of its constitutional window. See the Overview page for how this fits into the wider 2025–2028 state election cycle, and the Parties page for background on PH-DAP.
